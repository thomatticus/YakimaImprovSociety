Your Teacher

Meet Nicole!

Since 2016, Nicole has been helping people discover their most confident selves through the art of improv.

Her classes are a safe space filled with laughter, joy, and playfulness, where stepping out of your comfort zone feels exciting and fun.

With Nicole’s warm guidance and supportive teaching style, you’ll not only learn the art of improvisation, but also build confidence, creativity, and connection.
